Fly Welfare

Our Fly Welfare

At DeepEyeFlyAI, animal welfare is important. The flies used to train our model came from established research laboratories where fruit flies are commonly used for biological study. We did not raise or manage these flies ourselves. The images used for training were taken after the flies were put down whilst under anaesthetic gas.
